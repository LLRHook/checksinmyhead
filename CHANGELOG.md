# Changelog

Verified work is migrated here by `python3 .cycle/trackers.py migrate`, one line per
ticket: `- [<id>] <Jira key> <title> (Req: <ids>) [<bump>]`. The tracker entry is then
removed; Jira holds the full ticket and `trackers.py show <id>` recovers the entry.

The format is loosely [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/),
with `Verified`, `Added`, `Changed`, `Fixed`, `Removed`, and `Security`
sections under each release/date heading.
